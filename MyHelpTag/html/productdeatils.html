Description

Myhelptag Helmet Blood Group Sticker is a durable PVC sticker designed to provide essential emergency information. Along with your blood group, it can hold crucial details such as emergency contact and medical information, which you enter after registration and login. In case of an emergency, anyone assisting you can scan the sticker to access this vital information, helping doctors provide timely care. Additionally, the person who scans the tag can share their location for accurate assistance.
